# User Guide

## 1. Introduction

The CM Kerala Grievance Portal is a web-based platform that allows citizens to submit grievances, track their progress, and provide feedback.

It also provides dedicated dashboards for department officers and CMO administrators to manage and monitor grievances.

**Portal URL:**  
https://cm-kerala-grievance-portal-kappa.vercel.app

## 2. Citizen Guide

### 2.1 Citizen Registration

1. Open the portal.
2. Navigate to the registration option.
3. Enter the required personal details.
4. Submit the registration form.
5. Log in using the registered credentials.

### 2.2 Citizen Login

1. Open the citizen login page.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Submit the login form.
4. Access your citizen dashboard.

### 2.3 Submit a Grievance

1. Log in to your citizen account.
2. Open the grievance submission section.
3. Enter the required grievance details.
4. Select the relevant department.
5. Attach supporting documents if required.
6. Submit the grievance.

After successful submission, the system generates a grievance reference number.

Keep this reference number for future tracking.

### 2.4 Track a Grievance

1. Open the grievance tracking section.
2. Enter your grievance reference number.
3. Submit the tracking request.
4. View the current grievance status and available details.

Public tracking is available without logging in.

### 2.5 View Submitted Grievances

Citizens can log in to view their submitted grievances and monitor their progress through the citizen dashboard.

### 2.6 Submit Feedback

After a grievance is resolved:

1. Log in to your citizen account.
2. Open the relevant resolved grievance.
3. Access the feedback option.
4. Submit your feedback.

## 3. Department Officer Guide

### 3.1 Officer Login

1. Open the department officer login page.
2. Enter the assigned login credentials.
3. Submit the login form.
4. Access the officer dashboard.

### 3.2 View Assigned Grievances

Officers can view grievances assigned to their department and access the relevant grievance details.

### 3.3 Investigate a Grievance

1. Open an assigned grievance.
2. Review the citizen's submitted information.
3. Update investigation details.
4. Record the necessary progress.

### 3.4 Submit an Action-Taken Report

1. Open the relevant grievance.
2. Prepare the action-taken report.
3. Submit the report through the officer dashboard.
4. The report becomes available for CMO review.

## 4. CMO Administrator Guide

### 4.1 CMO Login

1. Open the CMO administrator login page.
2. Enter the authorized administrator credentials.
3. Submit the login form.
4. Access the CMO dashboard.

### 4.2 Dashboard Monitoring

The CMO dashboard provides an overview of grievance statistics and administrative activity.

Administrators can monitor grievance totals, pending cases, resolved cases, and other available dashboard information.

### 4.3 View and Review Grievances

1. Open the grievances section.
2. Browse the submitted grievances.
3. Select a grievance to view its details.
4. Monitor its progress and assigned department.

### 4.4 Assign Grievances

1. Select the grievance requiring assignment.
2. Choose the appropriate department and officer.
3. Submit the assignment.
4. The assigned officer can process the grievance through the department dashboard.

### 4.5 Review Action-Taken Reports

1. Open the relevant grievance.
2. View the department's submitted action-taken report.
3. Review the submitted information.
4. Submit the administrative review.

## 5. AI-Assisted Grievance Analysis

The portal integrates Google's Gemini API to support AI-assisted grievance analysis.

This functionality helps analyze submitted grievance information and supports the grievance-management process.

AI-generated information should be treated as assistance for administrative review, not as a replacement for human decision-making.

## 6. User Roles

| Role | Main Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Citizen | Register, submit grievances, track progress, and provide feedback. |
| Department Officer | Review assigned grievances, investigate cases, and submit action reports. |
| CMO Administrator | Monitor grievances, assign cases, and review departmental reports. |

## 7. Important Notes

- Keep your login credentials secure.
- Retain your grievance reference number.
- Submit accurate grievance information.
- Attach relevant supporting documents where necessary.
- Contact the appropriate support channel if you encounter problems using the portal.
